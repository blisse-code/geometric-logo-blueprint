# Changelog

## v2.1.0 (2026-11-13)

- Frame: for a ring-based mark, centre the ring on the crop; centre on the area centroid only when nothing echoes the crop.
- Tournament: place every round in the shared design file with scores before asking for a choice; shortlist by eye, revise each pick with its blueprint.
- Tests: context composites (real Dock, tab bar); a small-size cut for 16px; honest rasterising by pixel-centre sampling.
- Construction: pixel-grid option (cell = canvas/16); snap stems to a 16-unit grid; equal-sector gap rule for broken rings.
- Colour: one accent for one meaning, at least 3:1 for graphics.
- blueprint_kit.py: raster() samples pixel centres (the old fill added a false grey edge). The demo output changed accordingly.

## v2.0.0 (2026-11-13)

- Brief to constraints: turn adjectives into stroke, proportion, geometry, counter and terminal parameters; list category clichés.
- Variation tournament: 12 to 15 concepts across mark types, two gates, three finalists from different directions, one final. Keep every round.
- Test battery: measured shape and hole counts at 512, 32 and 16px; letter test after every weight or break change; mirror, rotation and squint; one-colour and reversed; shelf and familiarity.
- Optics written as numbers: optical-centre mass, overshoot ranges, a thinned reversed file, diagonal weight measured square to the stroke.
- Production: outlined arcs with few anchors, one path, `<title>`, a 24×24 single-path icon (Simple Icons format), a tight favicon crop, clear space from the mark's geometry, and a checkpoint before building the kit.
- Scorecard: crop and letter test become gates; adds distinction; craft and rebuild merged.
- New `scripts/blueprint_kit.py` with a demo.
- References: logo-design skill (Kaan Kızıltuğ), Simple Icons, Inkloom.
